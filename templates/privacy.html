Pixora provides browser-based and server-based tools for working with images. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use our website.

1. Information We Process

When you use Pixora, we may process images and files you choose to upload, along with the settings required to perform your requested operation.

Our hosting provider may also process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, timestamps, and error logs to operate and protect the website.

2. Image Uploads and Processing

Pixora processes uploaded images to provide the tool you request, such as conversion, resizing, cropping, or metadata editing. Images may be transmitted to our server for processing.

Do not upload confidential or sensitive images unless you are comfortable with the processing involved. We will document any third-party processing services here if we introduce them.

We do not claim that uploaded images are automatically deleted immediately in every circumstance. Temporary storage, logging, and retention depend on the implementation and hosting infrastructure.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Pixora or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for essential website functions, preferences, security, analytics, or advertising where enabled.

4. Advertising

If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads as permitted by their policies and applicable law.

Visitors can review Google's advertising and privacy information and manage available ad personalization settings through Google.

5. Third-Party Services

Pixora relies on hosting and infrastructure providers to operate the website. If we add analytics, advertising, donation, or external image-processing services, those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the website and the information processed through it. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Data is retained only as required for the relevant operational, legal, and security purposes, subject to the capabilities of our service providers.

7. Children's Privacy

Pixora is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should not have been collected, contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Contact us to make a request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Pixora's features and services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
